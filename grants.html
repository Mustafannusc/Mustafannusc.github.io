Grants & Funding

Research funding and proposal-development activities supporting computational materials science, nuclear materials, advanced manufacturing, and data-driven materials design.

Proposal Development Experience

  • Prepared and contributed to research proposals for federal and other competitive funding opportunities.
  • Developed technical scopes, computational work plans, milestones, and supporting research narratives.
  • Integrated molecular dynamics, multiscale simulation, machine learning, additive manufacturing, and experimental-validation concepts into proposal strategies.

Research Funding Themes

  • Radiation-tolerant and high-temperature structural materials
  • Oxide-dispersion-strengthened steels and advanced alloys
  • High-entropy and refractory multi-principal-element alloys
  • Atomistic and multiscale materials modeling
  • AI/ML-assisted materials design and property prediction
  • Additive manufacturing and extreme-environment materials

Funding & Proposal Roles

Proposal activities have included technical concept development, literature review, simulation-method design, project planning, and preparation of research narratives and supporting technical material.
