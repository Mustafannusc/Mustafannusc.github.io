Short Bio

I am a computational materials and nuclear engineering researcher specializing in atomistic and multiscale modeling of materials for extreme environments.

My research includes molecular dynamics simulations of irradiation damage, interfaces, oxide-dispersion-strengthened steels, high-entropy and refractory alloys, molten salts, and advanced structural materials for nuclear energy, aerospace, and high-temperature applications.

I received my Ph.D. in Nuclear Engineering and Technology and have held postdoctoral and visiting research appointments at Worcester Polytechnic Institute, Missouri University of Science and Technology, The University of Alabama, and Xi’an Jiaotong University.
