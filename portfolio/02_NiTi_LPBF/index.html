NiTi LPBF and Nanoparticle Consolidation

Physics-guided atomistic modeling of laser-induced heating, melting, consolidation, and interfacial evolution in NiTi nanoparticle systems.

Engineering Question

How do laser heating conditions, particle geometry, and thermal history control melting, consolidation, mixing, and interfacial evolution of NiTi particles on titanium substrates?

My Contribution

I developed a physics-guided atomistic workflow to study laser-induced nanoparticle heating and consolidation, including thermal-rate calibration, melt-state diagnostics, structural-order analysis, and processing descriptors intended for later integration with machine-learning models.

Methods

  • Molecular dynamics simulations
  • Temperature and heating-rate calibration
  • Melt-state indicators and structural-order analysis
  • Thermal-history and interface descriptors
  • Visualization of particle/substrate evolution
  • Physics-informed machine-learning integration

Engineering Outputs

  • Melting-onset and completion trends
  • Thermal-rate sensitivity
  • Structural-order evolution
  • Particle consolidation and interface descriptors
  • Processing-property features for data-driven models
