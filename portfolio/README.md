## Research Approach

My research connects atomic-scale mechanisms with engineering-scale performance through molecular dynamics, physically informed descriptors, machine learning, and experimental or literature-based validation. The objective is to develop interpretable processing-structure-property relationships that support materials selection and design for nuclear energy, aerospace, advanced manufacturing, and other extreme-environment applications.
