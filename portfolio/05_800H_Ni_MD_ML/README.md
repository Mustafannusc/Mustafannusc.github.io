# 800H/Ni Interfaces: Molecular Dynamics and Machine Learning

## Engineering Question
How do irradiation-induced defects and interface evolution affect the mechanical response of 800H/Ni systems, and can machine-learning models identify the dominant predictors?

## My Contribution
Integrated molecular-dynamics simulation outputs with physics-informed descriptors and machine-learning analysis to relate irradiation state, defect evolution, diffusion, and mechanical properties.

## Methods
- Molecular dynamics
- Irradiation and defect analysis
- Tensile deformation
- Dislocation analysis
- Diffusion calculations
- Feature engineering
- Regression / ensemble machine learning
- Cross-validation and replicated simulations

## Engineering Outputs
- Elastic and tensile-property trends
- Defect/mechanics relationships
- Feature-importance interpretation
- Predictive structure-property models

## Publication
Mechanical Response of Irradiated 800H/Ni Interfaces: Molecular Dynamics Simulations and Machine Learning Insights, Nuclear Instruments and Methods in Physics Research Section B, 580, 166275 (2026).
