HEA/RMPEA Alloy Design and High-Temperature Materials

Atomistic simulation and data-driven screening of compositionally complex alloys for high-temperature structural applications.

← Back to Research Portfolio

Engineering Question

How can atomistic simulations and data-driven models support composition screening and materials down-selection for high-temperature structural and manufacturing applications?

My Contribution

Contributed to computational studies of high-entropy and refractory multi-principal-element alloys, including composition-dependent mechanical behavior, high-temperature response, and screening relevant to experimental and additive-manufacturing development.

Methods

  • Molecular dynamics
  • Composition screening
  • Structure-property analysis
  • Mechanical-property prediction
  • Thermodynamic and structural descriptors
  • Scientific machine learning

Engineering Outputs

  • Candidate alloy ranking
  • Composition-property trends
  • Mechanical-response descriptors
  • Data for experimental down-selection
  • Frameworks connecting simulation with manufacturing decisions
