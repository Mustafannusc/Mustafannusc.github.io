Research Portfolio

Computational Materials Mechanics | Materials for Extreme Environments | AI-Enabled Materials Design

This portfolio highlights selected projects connecting atomistic simulation, mechanics, irradiation damage, high-temperature materials, advanced manufacturing, and scientific machine learning.

1. SiC/PyC/SiC Interface Mechanics and Irradiation

Atomistic modeling of interfacial adhesion, indentation, debonding, contact evolution, defect evolution, and irradiation response in SiC-based composite interfaces.

3. ODS Steels: Irradiation Damage and Nanomechanics
