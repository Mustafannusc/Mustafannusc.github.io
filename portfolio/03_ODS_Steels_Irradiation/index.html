ODS Steels: Irradiation Damage and Nanomechanics

Atomistic modeling of oxide-strengthened Fe systems under irradiation and mechanical loading.

← Back to Research Portfolio

Engineering Question

How do oxide nanoparticles, grain boundaries, voids, and dislocations influence radiation-damage evolution and mechanical response in Fe-based oxide-dispersion-strengthened steels?

My Contribution

Used atomistic simulations to quantify defect production, defect-interface interactions, dislocation-obstacle mechanisms, diffusion, and nanoindentation response in ODS-related Fe systems.

Methods

  • Molecular dynamics
  • Displacement-cascade simulations
  • Defect counting and clustering
  • Dislocation analysis
  • Nanoindentation
  • Diffusion calculations

Engineering Outputs

  • Defect-sink behavior
  • Dislocation-oxide interaction mechanisms
  • Irradiation-induced microstructural evolution
  • Nanoindentation response
  • Diffusion and grain-boundary effects
